0% to the platinum

All 55 trophies are offline single-player — this game has no online trophies. Spoiler mode hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them.

    Roadmap to the platinum

    About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online play, no mission replay. PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 difficulty. Keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter — there are 15 save slots and no way to replay a mission.

    Collectibles and counts — what the checklist asks for

    The 100% Completion checklist (Stage 2) is where the collectible and activity trophies live. The table in Stage 2 gives every count; the short version: 18 card sets (15 needed), 125 Cocaine Flamingos (100 needed), 18 meditation spots (15 for the checklist, all 18 for the trophy), 277 base-model vehicles (200 needed), 255 animals (100 studied), 397 stunts (300 needed), 80 Challenges (70 needed), 40 races (30 won), 42 shooting-range golds, 42 drone time trials, 15 Bret target decks, 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vaults, 33 underwater discoveries, 100 WAiNK locations, $250,000 laundered Marked Bills.

    1. Stage 1 — Story first 60–80 hours

      Play all five chapters with Jason and Lucia. Take optional missions as their icons appear (purple icons, and black icons with pink letters) — they feed the 100% checklist later. Scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app and study animals through your binoculars as you travel: the checklist wants 200 of 277 base-model vehicles and 100 of 255 animals, and progress made now already counts.

      Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before Chapter 1 ends — see Missables. It is the only trophy-related moment that can pass you by.

      Story trophies earned here: the 12 chapter and mission trophies, from Just Like Paradise to You Gotta Know When to Get Off (gold), plus the platinum's foundation.

    2. Stage 2 — The 100% Completion checklist about 100 hours

      After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. The checklist is closer to 60% true completion: most rows ask for a portion, so skip the rest. Rows that demand everything: all 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs, 8 bank vaults, 33 underwater discoveries, 42 shooting-range gold medals, 42 drone time trials and all 15 of Bret's target decks. Only 70 of the 80 Challenges are needed, so skip the ten hardest. Finish this stage and A Long Road (gold) pops.

    3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup 10–20 hours

      Finish the eight Strangers & Freaks strands (the five character finales — Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone — count toward the eight), sweep up the remaining meditation spots, and mop up any combat, driving, robbery and money trophies still open. When the last one pops, Vice Got a Price! follows.

    Missable trophies

    Only one trophy is potentially missable: Positive Mental Attitude. Everything else on the list either unlocks through the story or can be done in free roam after the story ends. There is no mission replay, so keep manual saves at the start and end of each chapter as a safety net. Cheats disable trophies and saving while active — keep them off this save.

    Save safety net — your insurance against the one miss

    There are 15 save slots and no mission replay, so spend a few of them on insurance. Save manually at the start and end of every chapter, and make a dedicated save in Chapter 1 right after the mission Down to Earth, before you do the balcony meditation — if anything goes wrong, that save is your way back. The 100% checklist only needs 15 of the 18 meditations, so even a missed balcony spot cannot cost you A Long Road; it can only cost you Positive Mental Attitude itself.

    Save safety — timed missions and boss fights

    • Never save in the middle of a timed mission (m7/m8). A save made while the timer is running reloads with the timer already ticking — and if the autosave overwrites your last clean slot mid-timer, you can be trapped in a run you cannot finish. Wait until the timer is over and you are back in free roam before you save.
    • Make a manual safehouse save before every boss mission. Sleep at a safehouse, then save in a fresh slot — never over your chapter-start save. If the fight goes wrong you reload to a calm street, not to the middle of the setup.
    • Keep one slot that is always "before the hard part". Rotate the other slots freely, but leave that one untouched until the mission is done — it is your way back if a checkpoint strands you with no ammo, no armour and no way out.

      Why nothing else can be missed

      The 12 story trophies unlock automatically as the chapters and named missions complete. Every collectible, activity, robbery and money trophy is available in free roam after the story, and the game never locks you out of Leonida. Some optional missions expire and a few fences disappear, but PowerPyx and KeenGamer both confirm none of that affects any trophy or the 100% checklist.
